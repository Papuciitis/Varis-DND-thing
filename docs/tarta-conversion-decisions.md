# Tarta conversion decisions

This add-on converts 32 printed creatures from the 79-page Homebrewery export, completes three partial summon templates, and completes the 13 previously imported wiki dragons. Source text, fixed source statistics and reviewed replacements remain in each YAML entry. All additions are optional homebrew content; they do not rewrite existing current spells or automatically grant class features.

## Collections and links

- `data/monsters/tarta-elementals-low.yaml`: 13 earth elementals from PDF pages 14–19.
- `data/monsters/tarta-elementals-high.yaml`: 13 earth elementals from pages 20–26.
- `data/monsters/tarta-summons.yaml`: six printed summon blocks plus Thorn Spitter and two caster-dependent duplicate templates.
- `data/monsters/tarta-wiki.yaml`: the 13 completed wiki dragons, regenerated by `scripts/import_tarta_monsters.py`.
- `data/draft-spells/tarta-homebrewery-summons.yaml`: ten optional Geomorphy spells.
- `data/draft-reference/tarta-summoning-features.yaml`: summon control plus four feature modules. Geomorphic Form links all 26 possible forms and labels them as form options, not simultaneous summons.
- `data/draft-reference/tarta-materials.yaml`: twelve material profiles, the configuration rule and Moissanite. Each context (golem, weapon and Bastion) is separate.

Forward creature references and reverse creation/control links use canonical names and groups. Monster edits and YAML exports keep the original source identity. Material previews are temporary configurations, not silent saved edits.

## Current combat rules

Attack rolls retain their source bonus. Ordinary Block DC equals that bonus plus 8. Attack damage and riders require an unblocked strike; automatic and saving-throw-only damage cannot be Blocked. Restrained becomes current Pinned with source escape methods preserved or explicitly supplied. Frightened becomes current Afraid. Reaction powers are placed in Reactions; Multiattack is one action entry. Recharge 5–6 is a d6 at the start of the creature’s turn while expended. Source rest-based powers use current rest durations, with daily recoveries remaining daily.

Printed fixed HP remains the playable baseline. Inconsistent dice or printed averages remain in source panels rather than being silently treated as new maximum HP. Attack dice are preferred over conflicting displayed averages. The source’s negative Charisma score for Voidstone Wanderer is repaired to 1, preserving the erroneous original separately. Variable duplicate templates retain caster-derived scores and CR Varies rather than fake exact values or invented XP.

Magnetite Guardian has no attacks. Resilient Plant has no actions or movement. The summoned Stone Servant follows its creating spell’s explicit cannot-attack rule and has utility Work/Help/Guard actions; the contradictory source Slam is archived.

## Common summon handling

A controlled creature shares its controller’s initiative and acts immediately afterward. The controller gives one verbal command on its own turn for no action cost; the creature spends its own normal actions and movement. Without orders it Dodges and avoids hazards. Parent spells specify range, active limit, duration and dismissal. Summons cannot recursively create copies or additional summons unless expressly allowed.

- Earth Elemental Servant: action plus tier-3-or-higher slot, once per long rest, one active servant, one hour.
- Primordial Geomancy: one ring, up to four Titans, one hour, once per long rest. Its personal Gargantuan form is distinct from the summons.
- Bloomguard: one stationary plant, 200 + casting modifier HP. The contradictory instantaneous/finite-duration source becomes one day at tier 5 and ten days at tier 6. The 100 gp seed is not consumed because the source does not say it is.
- Awakened Earth: 75 + casting modifier HP, anchored to the creating spell’s 50-foot-radius area; burrowing cannot transport it elsewhere. Multi-Assault makes a separate attack per target. It may choose Break control independently of orders or the Dodge default, at the GM’s discretion.
- Geochemical Golem: one active, concentration one hour; tier 6 extends to eight hours and concentration ends after the first uninterrupted hour. Missing burrow speed is completed as 30 feet.
- Stone Servant: one active, until dispelled, noncombat. Beyond 60 feet it stops acting until its controller returns; tier 6 adds 10 HP.
- Colossal Sand Golem: its name does not change its printed Huge size. Sand Form retaliation requires direct contact or an unblocked melee strike. Engulf gets DC 18 Dexterity avoidance, a Large-or-smaller limit and an action DC 18 Strength escape/rescue procedure. Release occurs on destruction/dismissal. Its parent is limited to one golem, concentration one hour.
- Thorn Spitter: a rooted plant chassis supplies AC 12, HP 27 and six scores; provisional CR 2. One action makes one caster-based ranged spell attack for 4d10 piercing, Block DC 8 + caster spell attack. No movement; one active, concentration one minute.
- Rockplica: half caster maximum HP, copied movement and combat statistics. Finite resources and remaining Block points are shared with the caster, not duplicated. It can provide verbal components but cannot maintain separate concentration or cast concentration spells while Rockplica already occupies the controller’s concentration. It cannot recursively copy or summon. One active copy, concentration one minute, 30-foot separation limit, action dismissal.
- Stone illusion: one HP, one minute, one active, 60-foot separation limit. It can move and gesture but cannot attack, cast or activate magical items.

## Material configurations

Use one to four different material choices. Base component cost is `max(1000 gp, sum of selected prices)`, plus 400 gp for a third and a further 2,000 gp for a fourth. The same choice never stacks with itself. This resolves the source’s minimum-mixture and itemized-price ambiguity. The selector starts unconfigured; casting requires at least one choice.

The golem preview lists only selected active effects and shows configured AC, melee attack/Block DC/damage modifiers and total components. Its immutable base block stays visible. Lanthara grants +2 AC; Holmara grants +2 melee attack, Block DC and damage. Other effects follow their linked material rules.

Missing golem infusion timings are explicit: Samarkium triggers at the start of the golem’s turn; Erbion takes an action for one touch heal per turn; Thulmara’s slowing aura has a defined start-of-turn trigger and ending; Dyspraxium is a bonus action until turn end and cannot end inside solid matter without damage/ejection; Terbithal uses DC 14 Constitution and blindness until the end of the target’s next turn after an unblocked Blast. Neodrakite moves only loose handheld metal objects up to 50 pounds each; anchored objects, structures and worn armor are excluded. Weapon/Bastion DC 16 rules remain distinct from the CR-5 golem’s DC 14 completions.

## Source spells above current tier 6

The current spell system ends at tier 6. Thorn Spitter (source 7), Treetimber (source 8) and Sandsoverign (source 9) become explicitly adapted tier-6 add-on spells. Their source levels remain in provenance. Sandsoverign gains one-golem/concentration limits, local creature creation and a ten-foot-cube-per-action inanimate shaping limit; its original mile-wide sand domain is terrain shaping, not an additional mile-wide automatic damage action. Stone Sentinel is a movable cover object, Nature’s Grasp is a Pinned effect, and Treetimber is instantaneous damage; none becomes an invented persistent monster.

## Wiki dragon completions

All 13 wiki profiles now have Bite/Claw/Tail, a one-bite/two-claw Multiattack, breath Recharge 5–6, Legendary Resistance and a three-point legendary pool. Added material-themed abilities are explicitly new homebrew completions informed by the material source, not recovered wiki text. Existing four numerical specialty sets preserve their damage, DCs and ranges. Aurumathar has bounded 120-foot bonus-action teleportation; Neodrakite’s breath specifies 10-foot displacement and half damage/no displacement on a successful save; Ytriun’s molten area has a finite duration.

The printed CRs are retained encounter references. These are playable conversions with documented decisions, not a claim that every source CR or high-level feature has been validated in live combat.
