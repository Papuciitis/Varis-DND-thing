# Tarta conversion decisions

This add-on converts 32 printed creatures from the 79-page Homebrewery export, completes three partial summon templates, and completes the 13 previously imported wiki dragons. Source text, fixed source statistics and reviewed replacements remain in each YAML entry. All additions are optional homebrew content; they do not rewrite existing current spells or automatically grant class features.

## Collections and options

- `data/monsters/tarta-elementals-low.yaml`: 13 earth elementals from PDF pages 14-19.
- `data/monsters/tarta-elementals-high.yaml`: 13 earth elementals from pages 20-26.
- `data/monsters/tarta-summons.yaml`: six printed creature blocks plus Thorn Spitter and two duplicate templates configured from a selected model creature.
- `data/monsters/tarta-wiki.yaml`: the 13 completed wiki dragons, regenerated by `scripts/import_tarta_monsters.py`.
- `data/draft-reference/tarta-creature-options.yaml`: **Tarta creature options**, in **Tarta Homebrewery - Creature options**, groups all 35 Homebrewery monsters and templates as optional creature choices.
- `data/draft-reference/tarta-materials.yaml`: twelve material profiles, the configuration rule and Moissanite. Each context (golem, weapon and Bastion) is separate.
- `data/geomancy-review/`: the complete original spell inventory in a separate **Geomancy spell review** section (`geomancy-review`), awaiting V.'s rework. Source levels 0-11, components, slots, durations and wording are preserved.

At the time of this conversion, the monster catalogue contained 160 entries; the later [recovered-monster import](recovered-monsters.md) brings it to 215. The current spell catalogue contains 439. The separate source-review section has 156 entries: 154 full spell bodies and two clearly marked index-only titles. It does not add spell choices to the current character builder, convert source rules automatically or grant class features. The page-40 index and body spelling differences are recorded in `data/geomancy-review/source-index.json`; [the review inventory](geomancy-spell-review.md) documents coverage and gaps.

Monster edits and YAML exports keep the original source identity. Material previews are temporary configurations, not silent saved edits. Selecting a creature card does not itself grant a way to create, summon or control that creature.

## Current combat rules

Attack rolls retain their source bonus. Ordinary Block DC equals that bonus plus 8. Attack damage and riders require an unblocked strike; automatic and saving-throw-only damage cannot be Blocked. Restrained becomes current Pinned with source escape methods preserved or explicitly supplied. Frightened becomes current Afraid. Reaction powers are placed in Reactions; Multiattack is one action entry. Recharge 5–6 is a d6 at the start of the creature’s turn while expended. Source rest-based powers use current rest durations, with daily recoveries remaining daily.

Printed fixed HP remains the playable baseline. Inconsistent dice or printed averages remain in source panels rather than being silently treated as new maximum HP. Attack dice are preferred over conflicting displayed averages. The source’s negative Charisma score for Voidstone Wanderer is repaired to 1, preserving the erroneous original separately. Variable duplicate templates use a selected model creature's scores and CR Varies rather than fake exact values or invented XP.

Magnetite Guardian has no attacks. Resilient Plant has no actions or movement. The utility Stone Servant retains the source's explicit noncombat design and has Work/Help/Guard actions; the contradictory source Slam is archived.

## Standalone monsters and optional controlled companions

Every creature can be used as a standalone encounter/NPC with normal turns and its listed actions. If the GM or an existing effect makes one a controlled companion, that effect or the GM determines duration, active limit, range, dismissal and any control cost. The statblock does not grant a new casting or creation ability. An optional shared-initiative/free-command convention applies only to a controlled companion; it must not make an independent NPC Dodge indefinitely because it has no controller.

- Earth Elemental Servant retains its printed combat profile; no new player spell slot cost, class level gate or creation feature is attached.
- Primordial Terra Titan is one separate monster option. The original four-Titan ring and the caster's personal transformation are historical feature context, not a new player ability.
- Resilient Plant is stationary, with no actions or attacks and a fixed defensive profile. A controlled variant can use an assigned controller modifier where its statblock specifies one. Original Bloomguard duration/upcasting text is preserved in the source-review section and does not replace this independent monster profile.
- Awakened Earth stays anchored to its assigned ground area; burrowing cannot transport it elsewhere. Multi-Assault makes a separate attack per target. If used as a controlled companion it may choose Break control independently of orders or a Dodge default, at the GM's discretion.
- Geochemical Golem has a completed 30-foot burrow speed and selectable material traits. Its existence is not dependent on a newly added Geochemical Golem spell.
- Stone Servant is a noncombat utility creature; its archived source spell does not appear in the current spell catalogue.
- Colossal Sand Golem keeps its printed Huge size. Sand Form retaliation requires direct contact or an unblocked melee strike. Engulf has DC 18 Dexterity avoidance, a Large-or-smaller limit and an action DC 18 Strength escape/rescue procedure. No Sandsoverign player spell is added.
- Thorn Spitter uses a rooted plant chassis: AC 12, HP 27 and six scores, with provisional CR 2. It makes one Thorn Spit per action for 4d10 piercing, using ranged spell attack +4 and Block DC 12. If an existing effect or the GM assigns a controller and calls for its casting statistics, use that controller's spell attack bonus instead and Block DC 8 plus that bonus. Its creature option does not grant a new spell.
- Stoneform Duplicate is a variable template rather than a fixed-CR creature. Select a model creature, including an NPC, to supply its recorded profile; it uses half that creature's maximum HP and copied combat statistics. Finite resources and remaining Block points are shared rather than duplicated. The duplicate gains no independent concentration capacity and cannot recursively copy or summon. Model statistics do not grant control. Its bound-duplicate rules retain the 30-foot separation limit; the GM or an existing effect can supply duration and control.
- Stone illusion duplicate is a noncombat template with 1 HP, configured from a selected model creature. It can move and gesture but cannot attack, cast or activate offensive magic items. Its optional controller setup does not publish the original rogue feature as a new player ability.

Specific standalone/setup rules and their documented completions live in each monster entry. Original spell levels, components and class-feature restrictions are kept in provenance for comparison only.

## Material configurations

Use one to four different material choices. The material budget preview is `max(1000 gp, sum of selected prices)`, plus 400 gp for a third and a further 2,000 gp for a fourth. The same choice never stacks with itself. This resolves the source’s minimum-mixture and itemized-price ambiguity. The selector starts unconfigured. Apply selected traits only when configuring that particular golem; the selector grants no casting ability.

The golem preview lists only selected active effects and shows configured AC, melee attack/Block DC/damage modifiers and total material budget. Its immutable base block stays visible. Lanthara grants +2 AC; Holmara grants +2 melee attack, Block DC and damage. Other effects follow their linked material rules.

Missing golem infusion timings are explicit: Samarkium triggers at the start of the golem’s turn; Erbion takes an action for one touch heal per turn; Thulmara’s slowing aura has a defined start-of-turn trigger and ending; Dyspraxium is a bonus action until turn end and cannot end inside solid matter without damage/ejection; Terbithal uses DC 14 Constitution and blindness until the end of the target’s next turn after an unblocked Blast. Neodrakite moves only loose handheld metal objects up to 50 pounds each; anchored objects, structures and worn armor are excluded. Weapon/Bastion DC 16 rules remain distinct from the CR-5 golem’s DC 14 completions.

## Original spell review

**Geomancy spell review** presents all original Geomorphy spell entries, including Bloomguard, Earth's Awakening, Rockplica, Thorn Spitter and Sandsoverign, for V. to select and adapt. The original levels 0-11 and older spell-slot rules remain visible as source text, with no current-tier remapping. These are review references outside the active spell catalogue and character builder. Source effects without an independent statblock, including Stone Sentinel, Nature's Grasp and Treetimber, remain spell effects rather than invented monsters. Selecting a review entry does not grant its spell or a class feature.

## Wiki dragon completions

All 13 wiki profiles now have Bite/Claw/Tail, a one-bite/two-claw Multiattack, breath Recharge 5–6, Legendary Resistance and a three-point legendary pool. Added material-themed abilities are explicitly new homebrew completions informed by the material source, not recovered wiki text. Existing four numerical specialty sets preserve their damage, DCs and ranges. Aurumathar has bounded 120-foot bonus-action teleportation; Neodrakite’s breath specifies 10-foot displacement and half damage/no displacement on a successful save; Ytriun’s molten area has a finite duration.

The printed CRs are retained encounter references. These are playable conversions with documented decisions, not a claim that every source CR or creature option has been validated in live combat.
