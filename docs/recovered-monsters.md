# Recovered monsters

55 additional editable profiles bring the manual from 160 to 215 entries. YAML remains the playable source of truth; no new player spells, class features or magic-item grants are added.

## Source and completion policy

The recovery used historical chat-search extracts, not a complete or verbatim transcript export. Each entry records the recovered name/date and known statistics under its collapsed source notes. Reconstructed scores, newly assigned encounter ratings and missing-rule completions are identified there. CR remains an encounter estimate after conversion.

Personal NPC names are replaced by reusable enemy archetypes with their distinctive weapons or body forms. Original aliases remain recorded in source notes and the inventory below. The two robotic cats retain their feline forms, recovered modifier arrays, HP, speed and attacks. The six-handed automaton remains stationary with six attacks.

## Current rules

- Natural armor does not grant equipment Block or training. Equipment uses named current armor and shields, Protection, legal hands/loadouts and combat floors.
- Weapon dice, range, Reload, Overdraw, Greataxe DC+ and Greatsword DC+++ follow current weapon entries; documented oversized monster dice remain specific exceptions.
- Strike damage and attached poison/grapple/control require an unblocked strike. Save-only areas, auras and automatic ongoing damage cannot be Blocked.
- Current Afraid, Snared, Confused, Madness and other conditions replace incompatible legacy wording. Daily and rest resources keep explicit timing; default short rest is one day and long rest one week.
- Summoned creatures are independent manual entries. Only an explicit creating ability supplies control, duration and initiative; spawn limits and shared recharge/action costs prevent recursive creation.
- Mud Servant shows immediately usable standalone defaults and the exact current spell scaling as an override. The existing fixed mud golem stays separate.
- Orog, War Chief and War Hulk use explicit shield/two-handed loadouts. A Versatile shield never permits an otherwise incompatible Greatsword beside it.
